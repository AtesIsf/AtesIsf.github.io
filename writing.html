ECON 352 Honors Paper

May 2026

An AS-AD analysis of the Greek debt crisis: can the AS-AD model explain Greece's 2009 crisis? Using the AS-AD and IS-LM models with FRED data from 2005 to 2019, I find that the predicted fall in output and prices largely holds. Rising real interest rates are the one mismatch, explained by collapsing investor confidence. The crisis also illustrates a constraint of a monetary union where member states rely on fiscal policy to counteract macroeconomic shocks.
